The shell of a site that hydrates no part of the shell: theme provider, skip link, header, page and footer. Part of the navigation family in VLLNT UI, it ships as a
machine-readable registry entry — copy the source directly into your app with
the shadcn CLI and own it, no runtime dependency on a component library.
