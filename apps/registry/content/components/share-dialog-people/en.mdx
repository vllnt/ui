Content panel of a people-sharing dialog: a share link, the people who have access with a role menu each, and an invite form. Part of the overlay family in VLLNT UI, it ships as a
machine-readable registry entry — copy the source directly into your app with
the shadcn CLI and own it, no runtime dependency on a component library.

Pass `people` and `roles`, then switch between the `list` and `invite` panels with `kind` and `onKindChange`. Set `linkUrl` to show the share link row. Wrap the panel in a `Dialog` or `Sheet` yourself: the host owns the data and every side effect, and the panel sends no invites and holds no permission logic. `layout` picks the `desktop` or `phone` density, `labels` overrides every visible and screen-reader string, and `onRemove` adds a Remove choice to each role menu.
