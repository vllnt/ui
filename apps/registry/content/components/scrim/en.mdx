Flat full-bleed overlay that dims the surface behind a panel, sheet or pane. Part of the overlay family in VLLNT UI, it ships as a
machine-readable registry entry — copy the source directly into your app with
the shadcn CLI and own it, no runtime dependency on a component library.

Set `level` to `dim` (the default, a light 50% black veil) or `strong` (an 80% black veil that hides what is behind it). The veil is black in every theme mode. It is decorative, so it renders with `aria-hidden` and screen readers skip it. It fills its nearest positioned ancestor; pass `className="fixed"` to cover the viewport.
