Tabbed content panels with keyboard-accessible tab triggers. Part of the navigation family in VLLNT UI, it ships as a
machine-readable registry entry — copy the source directly into your app with
the shadcn CLI and own it, no runtime dependency on a component library.

Pick the look of the triggers with the `variant` prop on `Tabs`: `underline` (the default) draws a rule under the list and marks the active tab with a primary underline, `pill` fills the active trigger with a rounded background, and `link` is plain text that underlines the active tab. `Tabs.List` and `Tabs.Trigger` read the variant from `Tabs`, so you set it once.
