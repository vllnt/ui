Asks the user to approve or deny one action, such as an agent running a command. It shows the risk and the status as words, the action in monospace and, while the request is pending, Approve and Deny buttons with an optional remember-my-choice checkbox. Three placements (`inline`, `hud` and `dialog`) change the surface only. Once the request is approved, denied or expired, the buttons go away and the status word stays. Part of the ai family in VLLNT UI, it ships as a
machine-readable registry entry — copy the source directly into your app with
the shadcn CLI and own it, no runtime dependency on a component library.
