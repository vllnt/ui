Highlighted quotation lifted out of long-form content: a short decorative rule, the quote and an optional attribution. It renders a semantic `figure` holding a `blockquote` and, when you pass `cite`, a `figcaption`. Two sizes, `md` and `lg`, scale the display type. The component is pure presentation: it composes no other component and reads no state. Part of the content family in VLLNT UI, it ships as a
machine-readable registry entry — copy the source directly into your app with
the shadcn CLI and own it, no runtime dependency on a component library.
